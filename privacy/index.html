Privacy Policy.

Your listening experience is personal. We aim to be clear about the information involved in providing Prunity Audio.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Prunity Inc. ("Prunity," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Prunity Audio mobile app and visit our website.

Prunity Audio currently does not require an account or sign-in. We ask for your email address and whether you have misophonia or know someone with misophonia. We use this information to support our community and improve Prunity. Live microphone audio is processed on your device. Other limited technical and usage information may be processed by service providers as described below.

2. Microphone and audio

Live audio processing

With your permission, Prunity Audio accesses your device's microphone to provide live noise reduction and listening features. Live microphone audio is processed on your device; it is not sent to Prunity's servers for live filtering. We do not record or store your live listening sessions.

Optional voice samples on your device

The app may let you deliberately create and manage short voice samples for voice-related features. If you choose to save these samples, they are stored locally on your device, rather than uploaded to Prunity's servers. They are separate from live listening sessions. You can manage these samples through available in-app controls.

Microphone permission can be changed through your device settings. Disabling access may prevent microphone-based features from working.

3. Information we process

Email address. We collect your email address when you submit the app's introductory questionnaire. We use it to manage your submission, respond to support or feedback requests, and for other purposes explained at the time of collection. Providing your email address does not automatically subscribe you to promotional emails.

Optional email updates. During onboarding, you may choose an optional checkbox that says: “I agree to receive emails from Prunity about app updates, new features, feedback opportunities, and community news.” If you select it, we may email you about those topics. You can use the app without opting in. You may withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe option in our emails or contacting support@prunityaudio.com. Withdrawing consent will not affect your ability to use Prunity.

Misophonia-related questionnaire answers. We ask whether you have misophonia. If you answer no, we ask whether you know someone with misophonia. We store your responses with your submitted email address to better understand the people interested in Prunity, improve the product, and guide community research and outreach. These answers may reveal information about your health or someone you know. We do not ask for the identity of the person you know, and a response is not a medical diagnosis. Please do not include someone else's identifying or medical information in your answers.

Storage. We use Supabase to store submitted email addresses and questionnaire answers. We also use the email communication preference submitted through onboarding to determine whether to send optional updates. Authorized members of our team may access these records for the purposes described in this policy. We do not publicly display your responses or sell them.

App usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the app is used, such as screens viewed, features enabled, and soundscapes selected. We do not require your name, email address, or an account for these analytics. Depending on SDK configuration, analytics can involve pseudonymous identifiers and technical information (such as device, app, and event information), and should not be assumed to be completely anonymous.

Messages you send us. If you email us for support or feedback, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message.

Website visits. When visiting our website, basic technical information may be handled by website hosting providers to deliver the site and maintain security. The website includes an optional audio demonstration that plays only when selected.

4. Third-party services

We use service providers to operate and improve Prunity Audio:

  • Supabase for secure storage of email addresses and questionnaire responses.
  • PostHog for app usage analytics.

These providers process information under their respective privacy practices. You can read the Supabase privacy policy, PostHog privacy policy.

Information processed by these providers may be stored or handled outside your province or country, including in the United States.

5. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to grant microphone access, and you can change that permission in your device settings. You can manage locally saved voice samples using the app's available controls. You can stop using the app and uninstall it; uninstalling generally removes locally stored app data, subject to your device's backup and operating-system behavior.

You can withdraw your consent to optional project-update emails at any time through the unsubscribe mechanism in those emails or by contacting us. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your email address, questionnaire answers, email preference, or other personal information that we control. Contact support@prunityaudio.com. We may use your email address to locate your questionnaire response and verify your request. Certain third-party or device-level data may need to be managed through those providers or your device.

6. Security and data retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with our services. Live audio processing stays on your device. Optional locally saved voice samples remain on your device unless you choose to remove them or device-level processes affect their storage.

We retain submitted email addresses and questionnaire responses while they are reasonably needed for Prunity community outreach, research and product improvement, unless you request deletion or a longer retention period is required by law. We retain support messages and service-related information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, applicable legal obligations, or legitimate business needs. Analytics records may be retained according to our service settings and providers' retention practices. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

7. Children's privacy

Prunity Audio is a general-audience product and is not specifically directed to children. The introductory questionnaire requests an email address and a misophonia-related answer. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children without any consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact support@prunityaudio.com so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Prunity Audio evolves. For example, if we introduce user accounts or optional audio uploads or cloud processing, we will revise this policy before those practices begin and provide notice or request consent when required. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Prunity Inc. at support@prunityaudio.com.
